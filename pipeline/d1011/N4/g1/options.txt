Rows: G1 = raw features_v3 ic_noise >= 9.5 and ic_ridge_snr < 0.5 (208 train; test midLF 113 + G1hi 320).

Recipe of one option (het spec h, stretch step s), as q7_fsim_test.py / posthoc/nested_select_g1.py:
  r = y - blend_v21 nested OOF; hr = het - g(logN) (g linear, fitted on fitting-fold G1 rows, per estimator);
  offset = OLS (no intercept) of (r - mean r) on hr over fitting-fold G1 rows; G1 rows = v21 + offset; v27u elsewhere;
  then on the G1hi low half: c + k (p - c), c = mean base OOF, b = OLS slope (with intercept) of y on base OOF over the
  fitting-fold low-half rows, k = 1 + s (b / RHO - 1), RHO = 0.9795141681843882.  (s = 0.5 is v28n's half step.)
Het specs H (6), estimators: LOC = mean(ev2s_loc_e32r4, ev2s_loc_e32r4_s1); A2 = ev2s_loc_fsim; A3 = ev2s_locK_real5;
  A23 = mean(A2, A3) (het and logN averaged):
  H1 LOC            (v28n estimator)
  H2 replace_A2     (offset from A2 only)
  H3 replace_A3     (offset from A3 only)
  H4 replace_A23    (offset from A23 only)
  H5 avg_A23        (het = mean(LOC het, A23 het), g on LOC logN, one beta)
  H6 twobeta_A23    (columns LOC and A23, two betas)
  A1 (AdaBN) is excluded: closed in section 14 (gate pc 0.995, near copy of LOC) and its test half is not being dumped
  (laptop section 15 dumps only A2/A3 test halves). avg/twobeta only for A23 to keep the set <= 20.
Stretch steps S (3): 0.5 (v28n), 0.75, 1.0.

PROCEDURE g1 (main): 18 options = H x S, list order H1..H6 outer, S inner:
  LOC/0.5, LOC/0.75, LOC/1.0, replace_A2/0.5, ..., twobeta_A23/1.0.  LOC/0.5 = no change (reproduces v29g exactly).
PROCEDURE g1_stretch (secondary, buildable now; no A2/A3): 3 options LOC/0.5, LOC/0.75, LOC/1.0.

  Assert LOC/0.5 mapped OOF == v29g OOF (max abs < 1e-9) and LOC/0.5 mapped test == blend_v29g.csv on all rows.
Selection-honest nested OOF: outer folds = data/folds.csv. For outer fold k, every option is scored by inner CV on the
  outer-training rows only (inner folds = the 4 other outer folds; for inner fold j every fit uses rows of folds not in
  {j, k}); score = MSE of the v29g-mapped candidate over the outer-training rows; pick = argmin (ties -> earlier in list
  order). The picked option then predicts fold k with fits on folds != k (the fixed-option nested recipe).
  Convention kept from v28n (needed for the exact reproduction): the stretch's c and b for fold k are computed from the
  base OOF of the other folds' rows (each built on its own other folds), as v28n's a_test did with the v27u OOF.
